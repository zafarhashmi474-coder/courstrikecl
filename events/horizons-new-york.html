Hashim Syed

AI Leader

Hashim Syed is the AI Go-to-Market Lead for Startups in North America at Google, where he helps emerging technology companies scale with AI and cloud solutions. He focuses on shaping strategic partnerships, driving adoption of AI-enabled tools, and supporting founders as they bring innovative products to market in a competitive landscape.

Sebastian Misiurek

Senior Product Manager

Sebastian Misiurek is a Senior Product Manager at NVIDIA, where he leads cloud-native Digital Twin and Physical AI products within the Omniverse ecosystem. He works across engineering, design, and enterprise customer teams to transform complex AI and 3D simulation capabilities into intuitive tools. Driven by computational workflows, he also serves as a Visiting Professor at Pratt Institute.

Hakarl Bee

Creative Lead - AI Design

Hakarl Bee is the Creative Lead of AI Design at Citi, where he directs the evolution of the firm's brand identity for the AI age. He focuses on building a comprehensive brand expression spectrum and establishing the governance tools that ensure brand quality within Citi's agentic AI platforms, seamlessly scaling an AI-enabled design system across consumer, employee, and enterprise touchpoints.

Christi Fisher

Global Head of Product and Process Transformation

Christi Fisher is the Head of Product Transformation & Operations at BNY, where she leads global enterprise transformation across products, technology, and service experiences. She focuses on developing AI-enabled products, modernizing operations, and integrating intelligent workflows across complex business environments. Bringing over 20 years of experience from JPMorgan Chase, she combines product strategy with hands-on leadership to transform fragmented ecosystems into scalable solutions.

Michael K. Wagner

Vice President, Product Excellence

Michael K. Wagner is the Vice President of Product Excellence at Mastercard, where he leads enterprise-wide initiatives to fundamentally improve how the organization builds and manages products at scale. He focuses on transforming the core product operating model, optimizing governance, decision-making, and lifecycle management to seamlessly connect overarching business strategy and investment with product execution and tangible business outcomes.

Dorothy Joseph

Executive Director, Head of Wealth Management Digital Marketing Applications

Dorothy Joseph is the Executive Director and Head of Wealth Management Digital Marketing Applications at Morgan Stanley, where she specializes in driving the digital strategy, technology roadmap, and implementation of advanced marketing cloud solutions. She focuses on aligning complex marketing technology ecosystems with overarching wealth management objectives, empowering the firm to deliver targeted messaging, robust analytics, and personalized digital experiences to its clients.

Radek Meljon

Vice President

Radek Meljon is a Vice President at Courstrike, the global leader in digital and AI skills training for teams of professionals with locations in New York, London and Toronto. Courstrike works directly with industry leaders to design and deliver innovative training programs that equip professionals with the in-demand skills needed to succeed in today's technology-driven economy and has trained over 35,000 professionals to date.

Anna Baird

Chief Customer Officer, Generative AI GTM

You wouldn't think of brand leadership as a technical discipline. Anna Baird unpacked how generative AI is reshaping the way organizations engage customers, make decisions, and scale trust. Drawing from boardroom experience at Google, she highlighted the importance of emotional intelligence, responsible AI, and human-centred thinking as the true differentiators in an AI-powered era.

Sarah Bishop

VP, Global Brand and Consumer Marketing

The NFL is expanding its global reach through inclusive brand strategy, culturally attuned campaigns, and next-generation audience engagement. Drawing from nearly two decades across sports, media, and tech, Sarah Bishop highlighted how strategic partnerships, fan-first storytelling, and purpose-driven initiatives grow affinity, recruit new audiences, and deepen engagement across generations.

Chris Walker

Research Lead

Research shapes how Cash App makes financial systems more accessible. Drawing on market, policy, and behavioral insights, Chris Walker showed how data guides products that empower first-time savers, debit card holders, and everyday investors. He highlighted how understanding financial barriers, building trust, and designing for inclusion drive impact across Cash App's ecosystem.

Liz Bergman

Vice President & GM

Liz Bergman shared how American Express leverages strategy, operations, and AI to drive customer-centric growth. She highlighted how cross-functional alignment and thoughtful product launches create seamless experiences, how data informs decisions, and how brands can scale while maintaining trust and human connection in an increasingly automated world.

Ankur Jain

Founder & CEO

Ankur Jain shared how Bilt transforms rent, a major financial commitment, into opportunities for wealth, rewards, and long-term value. Drawing on his experience building a multi-billion-dollar platform, he explored mission-driven growth, designing products without a finished image, and solving overlooked but critical problems in fintech and real estate.
