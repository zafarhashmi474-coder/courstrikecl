THEMES BEING EXPLORED:

Leading Product Strategy Through Disruption

Frameworks for navigating market uncertainty, maintaining cross-functional alignment, and building resilient product roadmaps in rapidly evolving industries.

Building Trust in AI-Driven Products

Best practices for integrating intelligent systems and autonomous features while prioritizing user confidence, data privacy, and transparent user experiences.

Unlocking Product-Led Growth and Retention

How to build self-sustaining ecosystems by combining continuous user discovery, community engagement, and operational discipline to drive enduring product growth.

"Shifting Left" in the Product Lifecycle

Practical frameworks for accelerating time-to-value and reducing risk by bringing continuous discovery, technical feasibility, and quality assurance to the earliest stages of development.

The Product Manager of the Future

How autonomous agents, large language models, and advanced tooling are reshaping product workflows, strategic decision-making, and the PM's role in human-computer interaction.

Speakers from Past Events

Our stage hosts senior leaders from global brands who are driving innovation at scale.

Anna Baird

Chief Customer Officer, Generative AI GTM

You wouldn't think of brand leadership as a technical discipline. Anna Baird unpacked how generative AI is reshaping the way organizations engage customers, make decisions, and scale trust. Drawing from boardroom experience at Google, she highlighted the importance of emotional intelligence, responsible AI, and human-centred thinking as the true differentiators in an AI-powered era.

Sarah Bishop

VP, Global Brand and Consumer Marketing

The NFL is expanding its global reach through inclusive brand strategy, culturally attuned campaigns, and next-generation audience engagement. Drawing from nearly two decades across sports, media, and tech, Sarah Bishop highlighted how strategic partnerships, fan-first storytelling, and purpose-driven initiatives grow affinity, recruit new audiences, and deepen engagement across generations.

Chris Walker

Research Lead

Research shapes how Cash App makes financial systems more accessible. Drawing on market, policy, and behavioral insights, Chris Walker showed how data guides products that empower first-time savers, debit card holders, and everyday investors. He highlighted how understanding financial barriers, building trust, and designing for inclusion drive impact across Cash App's ecosystem.

Liz Bergman

Vice President & GM

Liz Bergman shared how American Express leverages strategy, operations, and AI to drive customer-centric growth. She highlighted how cross-functional alignment and thoughtful product launches create seamless experiences, how data informs decisions, and how brands can scale while maintaining trust and human connection in an increasingly automated world.

Ankur Jain

Founder & CEO

Ankur Jain shared how Bilt transforms rent, a major financial commitment, into opportunities for wealth, rewards, and long-term value. Drawing on his experience building a multi-billion-dollar platform, he explored mission-driven growth, designing products without a finished image, and solving overlooked but critical problems in fintech and real estate.

Courstrike's Product Summit is an exclusive, semi-annual conference designed for professionals driving the future of digital products, technology, and business. This intensive, single-day event convenes a curated group of executives and industry leaders to explore the most relevant ideas shaping technology and digital products, and to anticipate what's next. Each edition of Product Summit features expert panel discussions, dynamic product demonstrations, and strategic networking, where attendees can gain insights and meaningful connections in an intimate, high-impact event.

The summit runs from 3:00 PM to 7:00 PM EST, featuring consecutive keynote talks, tactical executive panels, interactive technology deep-dives, and concludes with a cocktail networking reception.
