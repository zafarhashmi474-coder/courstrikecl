Artificial Intelligence Course Curriculum

UNIT 1

AI Foundations & Prompt Engineering

Build the core AI skills professionals need to work smarter, faster, and more effectively with modern AI tools. In Unit 1, you'll explore how AI systems work, including large language models (LLMs), neural networks, and machine learning fundamentals. Through hands-on exercises and real-world business scenarios, you'll develop prompt engineering skills that help you generate more accurate, relevant, and effective AI outputs while exploring the ethical and governance considerations shaping AI adoption.

Understand AI Technologies

Develop a practical understanding of the technologies powering modern AI systems, including machine learning, neural networks, generative AI, and large language models. Learn how AI systems process data, generate outputs, and create business value across industries.

Hands-On Prompt Engineering

Learn how to write effective prompts that improve AI-generated outputs. Practice working with AI tools to understand how prompt structure, context, and specificity influence results.

Ethics, Governance & Responsible AI

Explore the ethical, legal, and governance considerations surrounding AI adoption. Learn how organizations address bias, transparency, privacy, and responsible AI implementation.

UNIT 2

Advanced Prompt Engineering

Move beyond one-off prompts and learn how to build repeatable AI systems. In Unit 2, you'll explore advanced prompting techniques, prompt chaining, and multi-step AI interactions that improve consistency, scalability, and execution across professional workflows. Learn how to break complex problems into structured atomic steps while building reusable AI skills that can be adapted across projects, teams, and business functions.

Advanced Prompt Engineering Techniques

Learn advanced prompting methods that improve reasoning, structure, context handling, and output quality. Develop prompts that support more complex business tasks.

Prompt Chaining & AI Pipelines

Learn how to connect prompts into structured, multi-step workflows for a range of professional scenarios. Explore how prompt chains help move AI work from isolated outputs to repeatable processes.

Atomic Thinking

Break complex business problems into atomic tasks to improve AI reliability, workflow design, and output quality. Learn how structured decomposition improves output quality and decision-making.

Build Reusable AI Skills

Build repeatable AI systems that can be adapted across roles, projects, and business functions. Learn how to package prompt chains, instructions, context, and evaluation criteria into reusable AI skills that support scalable, consistent AI-assisted workflows.

From there, you'll explore AI agents, autonomous systems capable of reasoning through objectives, coordinating actions, and executing multi-step workflows. Through hands-on exercises and real-world scenarios, build AI workflows and agents to scale execution, automate processes, and transform the way work gets done.

This course builds practical, applied AI skills you can use immediately in your work. You will move from foundations to hands-on application across three areas:

Foundations & Prompt Engineering

Advanced Prompt Engineering

AI Workflows & Agents
