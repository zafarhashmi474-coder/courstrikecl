The Global Leader in AI & Digital Skills Training

BrainStation's global spaces are custom-built for real learning, deep collaboration and building connections. Located in key cities, our spaces bring industry professionals together to learn and build.

New York

Located in the heart of New York's vibrant tech and creative community, BrainStation New York brings professionals together to learn, collaborate, and build.

London

Based in the tech district of Shoreditch, BrainStation is at the heart of London's digital scene, surrounded by the most cutting-edge companies in the city. In addition to courses, BrainStation London offers training sessions, industry events, expert panel discussions, and more.

Toronto

Located in Toronto's vibrant technology community, BrainStation Toronto provides a modern space where professionals can learn, connect, and build new skills.
